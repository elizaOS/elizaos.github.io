# Overall Project Weekly Summary (Jul 26 - 1, 2026)

## Executive Summary
This week, the ElizaOS team focused on hardening our core infrastructure to ensure a stable, reliable foundation for our growing agent ecosystem. By resolving critical production issues and streamlining our development processes, we have improved the framework's resilience while making significant progress on the new "Agentic Parent Suite" for household coordination.

**Strengthening Platform Reliability**
_Goal: We aimed to eliminate production errors and ensure that our agents operate consistently and predictably._
- Resolved critical failures in agent management, ensuring that agents can be created and removed without system errors ([elizaos/eliza](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17260)).
- Improved the accuracy of agent monitoring and data tracking, ensuring that user interactions and wallet balances are recorded correctly ([elizaos/eliza](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17140)).
- Optimized our internal testing and deployment pipelines to prevent system crashes and speed up development workflows ([elizaos/eliza](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17238)).

**Expanding Agent Capabilities**
_Goal: We are building new features that allow agents to handle complex, real-world tasks like household management and on-chain execution._

- Began integrating the Aomi plugin, which will enable agents to perform more sophisticated on-chain executions ([elizaos/eliza](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17288)).
