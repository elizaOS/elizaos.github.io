# ElizaOS Daily Summary: July 1, 2026

On July 1, 2026, the ElizaOS team focused on hardening the framework’s security and expanding the Eliza Cloud platform’s commercial capabilities. Key achievements include the implementation of advanced PII pseudonymization, the launch of new monetization and marketplace primitives, and significant UI/UX refinements to the desktop and chat experience.

## ✅ Completed Work

### Security & Privacy Hardening
*   **PII Protection:** Implemented NER-driven pseudonymization at the model boundary and expanded secret-swap detection to safeguard sensitive user data ([elizaos/eliza#10779](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10779), [elizaos/eliza#10735](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10735)).
*   **Operational Safety:** Hardened sensitive request fields and added structured confirmation for destructive actions to prevent unauthorized operations ([elizaos/eliza#10840](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10840), [elizaos/eliza#10677](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10677)).

### Eliza Cloud Platform & Monetization
*   **Monetization Infrastructure:** Launched ad inventory/SSP primitives for miniapps and an influencer marketplace with escrow support, backed by secure Stripe and OxaPay payout rails ([elizaos/eliza#10942](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10942), [elizaos/eliza#10951](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10951), [elizaos/eliza#10863](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10863)).
*   **Agent Management:** Enabled custom domain management and robust app configuration backup/restore functionality ([elizaos/eliza#11000](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11000), [elizaos/eliza#10983](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10983)).

### UI/UX & Desktop Experience
*   **Interface Refinements:** Enhanced the desktop experience with a new notification center, frosted-glass chat overlays, and programmable hotkeys ([elizaos/eliza#10669](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10669), [elizaos/eliza#10661](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10661), [elizaos/eliza#10826](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10826)).
*   **Customization & Control:** Introduced versioned undo/redo for app backgrounds and improved widget aesthetics ([elizaos/eliza#10771](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10771), [elizaos/eliza#10737](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10737)).

### Orchestration & Stability
*   **System Performance:** Integrated a PTY service for CLI access and implemented performance regression guardrails for view-bundle sizes ([elizaos/eliza#10668](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10668), [elizaos/eliza#10861](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/10861)).
