# Overall Project Summary: July 31, 2026

Development on July 31 focused on stabilizing cloud infrastructure and refining agent coordination, specifically resolving critical production agent deletion failures and implementing multi-agent response management. The team also successfully integrated the `eliza-app` frontend into the monorepo, significantly streamlining the project's UI/UX deployment pipeline.

## 🚨 Needs Attention 
- **[elizaos/eliza#17421](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17421):** Ongoing I/O starvation and port collisions on self-hosted runners require continued monitoring and potential infrastructure adjustments.
- **[elizaos/eliza#13562](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/13562):** The nightly "Views Soak" test continues to fail; investigation into potential render-storms or memory growth in the `ViewRouter` is required.
- **[elizaos/eliza#17136](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17136):** The `lifeops-connectors` shard remains a performance bottleneck that requires further sub-splitting to resolve nightly test failures.

## ✅ Completed Work

### Cloud Infrastructure & Reliability
*   Resolved critical production agent deletion failures by removing redundant timestamp operations ([elizaos/eliza#17249](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17249)).
*   Improved job recovery and capacity management by ensuring dependent rows settle correctly and preventing stranded warm-pool rows ([elizaos/eliza#17442](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17442), [elizaos/eliza#17286](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17286)).
*   Fixed a bug in the orphan reaper that was incorrectly deleting live warm-claimed containers ([elizaos/eliza#17256](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17256)).

### Agent Coordination & Safety
*   Implemented P4 group-room coordination to prevent multiple agents from responding to the same Discord message ([elizaos/eliza#17213](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17213)).
*   Enhanced agent provenance and safety by introducing a canonical provenance envelope and enforcing single-voice delivery contracts ([elizaos/eliza#17304](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17304), [elizaos/eliza#17478](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17478)).

### Frontend & UI/UX
*   Ported the `eliza-app` frontend into the monorepo Vite homepage and deployed to Cloudflare ([elizaos/eliza#17376](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17376)).
*   Applied visual refinements including iOS chat glass effects and a new default "Ember Night" wallpaper ([elizaos/eliza#17048](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17048), [elizaos/eliza#17454](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17454)).
