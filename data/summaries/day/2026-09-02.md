# Overall Project Summary: Sep 2, 2026

Development on September 2, 2026, focused on stabilizing cloud infrastructure and optimizing inference admission within the `elizaos/eliza` repository. Key achievements include the successful deployment of a new subscription funding taxonomy and significant latency reductions in proxy operations, alongside the promotion of certified develop builds to production.

## ✅ Completed Work

**Cloud Billing & Subscription Lifecycle**
*   Established a new subscription funding taxonomy to distinguish between allowance-eligible and cash-only usage ([elizaos/eliza#30165](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30165)).
*   Optimized resource management by enabling the reservation of subscription allowances prior to credit utilization ([elizaos/eliza#30166](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30166)).
*   Enhanced billing transparency and auditability by persisting lifecycle aggregates and adding reporting for uncollected overages ([elizaos/eliza#30154](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30154), [elizaos/eliza#30167](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30167)).

**Inference Admission & Proxy Optimization**
*   Reduced inference latency by streamlining pre-auth rate limiting and optimizing organization-level gating ([elizaos/eliza#30351](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30351)).
*   Improved security and efficiency by fusing credential and balance admission, and guarding paid proxy routes ([elizaos/eliza#30265](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30265), [elizaos/eliza#30264](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30264)).

**Infrastructure Stability & CI/CD**
*   Improved system reliability by isolating Dedicated startup from Shared runtime environments ([elizaos/eliza#29973](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29973)).
*   Restored integration contracts and successfully promoted certified develop build 769d433 to production ([elizaos/eliza#30363](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30363), [elizaos/eliza#30366](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30366)).
