# ElizaOS Daily Summary: July 23, 2026

Development on July 23 focused on stabilizing the ElizaOS framework through critical cloud infrastructure hardening and performance optimizations for chat latency. Key achievements include the promotion of canary controls to production, the unification of intent routing, and the delivery of native LifeOps features like Cloud Notes and Calendar.

## ✅ Completed Work

**Cloud Infrastructure & Performance**
*   Promoted exact-agent canary controls to production and optimized cloud inference admission and billing paths to reduce latency ([elizaos/eliza#17144](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17144), [elizaos/eliza#16929](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16929), [elizaos/eliza#17076](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17076), [elizaos/eliza#16982](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16982), [elizaos/eliza#16925](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16925)).
*   Implemented secure warm-pool key handoffs and character pushing to ensure claimed agents are immediately functional upon deployment ([elizaos/eliza#16977](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16977), [elizaos/eliza#17066](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17066), [elizaos/eliza#17078](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17078), [elizaos/eliza#17083](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17083)).

**UI/UX & Intent Routing**
*   Unified structural intent vocabulary for chat, voice, and transcription, and implemented a typed, append-only transcript-event contract for consistent cross-platform rendering ([elizaos/eliza#16510](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16510), [elizaos/eliza#16507](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16507)).
*   Improved navigation and focus by enhancing the home notification shade and wallet balance surface ([elizaos/eliza#16918](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16918), [elizaos/eliza#16956](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16956)).

**Core Runtime & LifeOps**

*   Improved planner reliability by transitioning to capability-based matching instead of hardcoded action names ([elizaos/eliza#16749](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/16749)).
