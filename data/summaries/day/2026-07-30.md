# Overall Project Summary (Jul 30, 2026)

The project reached a major milestone today with the promotion of `develop` to `main` (release #17300), which stabilized Tier-0 shared agent startup probes and resolved critical cloud deployment errors. Development efforts were heavily focused on establishing the "LifeOps" parent-assistant architecture and hardening the CI/CD pipeline to eliminate persistent regressions.
