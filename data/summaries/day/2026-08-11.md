# ElizaOS Daily Digest: August 11, 2026

August 11, 2026, was defined by significant infrastructure hardening and stabilization of the ElizaOS framework, with a primary focus on optimizing cloud agent lifecycle management and CI/CD reliability. The team successfully improved production stability through targeted bug fixes in agent pairing, voice integration, and Discord onboarding processes.

## ✅ Completed Work

**Cloud Infrastructure & Agent Lifecycle**
*   Scaled cloud capacity and improved agent creation routing to support launch-chain requirements ([elizaos/eliza#18351](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18351), [elizaos/eliza#18362](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18362), [elizaos/eliza#18364](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18364)).
*   Restored managed dedicated-agent pairing and resolved credential-boundary and inventory termination issues ([elizaos/eliza#18305](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18305), [elizaos/eliza#18137](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18137), [elizaos/eliza#18350](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18350)).

**Voice, Media, and Core Logic**
*   Enhanced voice and media capabilities, including iOS system voice integration with Cartesia and improved transcription/URL routing ([elizaos/eliza#18318](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18318), [elizaos/eliza#18376](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18376), [elizaos/eliza#18429](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18429), [elizaos/eliza#18348](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18348)).
