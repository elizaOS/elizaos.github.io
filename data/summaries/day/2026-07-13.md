# Overall Project Summary: Jul 13, 2026

Development on July 13 focused on hardening the ElizaOS framework through improved orchestrator reliability, robust telemetry, and the replacement of synthetic mocks with real service integrations. While significant progress was made in cloud infrastructure and testing parity, the team is currently addressing critical routing challenges in the staging agent mesh and stabilizing the nightly E2E suite.
