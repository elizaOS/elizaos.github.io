The goal is to ensure that `DISCORD_DEFAULTS` are respected uniformly, preventing fragmented behavior between voice and text interactions.
*   **Relevant PRs:** 
    *   [#50](https://github.com/elizaos-plugins/plugin-discord/pull/50): Opened to ensure the `DISCORD_AUTO_REPLY` environment variable is correctly integrated and respected via `DISCORD_DEFAULTS`.
