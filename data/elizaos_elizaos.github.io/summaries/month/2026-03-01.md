# elizaos/elizaos.github.io Monthly Report (March 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
During March 2026, development focused on maintaining the project's underlying infrastructure and fostering discussions around the integration of AI-driven marketplaces. This issue serves as a focal point for discussing how agents can autonomously handle financial transactions, a critical component for the project's mission of decentralization and autonomy.
