# elizaos/eliza Weekly Report (Jul 26 - 1, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week, the ElizaOS development cycle was defined by a rigorous focus on production stability and the hardening of cloud infrastructure. The team successfully resolved critical failures in agent deletion and provisioning workflows, while simultaneously advancing the "Agentic Parent Suite" initiative to enable sophisticated household coordination. Through comprehensive dependency updates and CI/CD optimizations, the project has significantly improved its resilience, ensuring that the framework remains robust as it scales across Web2 and Web3 ecosystems.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Cloud Infrastructure & Production Stability
The primary technical focus was stabilizing the managed agent lifecycle.
*   **Lifecycle Management:** Addressed a P0 production issue where agent deletion retries failed due to timestamp type mismatches ([#17260](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17260), [#17251](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17251)).
*   **Diagnostic Tools:** Implemented privacy-safe profiling for canary deletion errors and introduced read-only diagnostic workflows for staging environments to inspect stale states without triggering mutations ([#17189](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17189), [#17196](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17196)).
*   **Provisioning:** Fixed issues with agent startup probes and sweeper logic that incorrectly flagged legitimate jobs as errors ([#17292](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17292)).

### Agent Runtime & Telemetry
*   **Streaming & Persistence:** Unified the chat streaming pipeline (provider to database) to ensure telemetry accuracy and refined runtime ownership for assistant-message persistence ([#17140](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17140)).
*   **Action Logic:** Enhanced action callback telemetry to improve attribution and updated wallet balance-delta logic to handle price-coverage flips more deterministically ([#17248](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17248), [#17137](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17137)).

### CI/CD & Dependency Management
*   **Pipeline Hardening:** Resolved deterministic failures in Windows CI and optimized performance by bounding concurrency and managing cache sizes ([#17238](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17238), [#17225](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17225)).
*   **Dependency Sweep:** Executed a comprehensive update of core development dependencies, including Biome, Playwright, and Turbo, to maintain environment parity ([#17291](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17291)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
*   **Agent Coordination:** Issue [#17204](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17204) regarding agent project coordination was successfully resolved.

### New & Active Issues
*   **Agentic Parent Suite:** The "Parent Suite" epic ([#17175](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17175)) remains a major area of active development, with ongoing work to modularize calendar ingestion, household roles, and scheduling approvals.
*   **Persistent Blockers:** The "Nightly App Live E2E" ([#13681](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/13681)) remains the most significant blocker, requiring three consecutive green runs to close. Similarly, the Android device E2E ([#16263](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/16263)) is pending verification on API-34 host-backed runners.
*   **New Integrations:** New issues were opened to track the integration of the Aomi on-chain execution plugin ([#17288](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17288)).

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Collaboration this week was heavily focused on the "Agentic Parent Suite," where contributors are actively coordinating to split the workload into independent, reviewable segments. The high volume of PRs related to CI/CD and dependency management indicates a strong, ongoing effort to maintain project health. The introduction of a builder bounty ([#17201](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17201)) and the community-driven Ruby High plugin ([#17174](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17174)) highlight a healthy, expanding ecosystem of contributors working alongside core maintainers.
