# elizaos/eliza Weekly Report (Aug 9 - 15, 2026)

## 🚀 Highlights
The week of August 9–15, 2026, was defined by a major push toward cloud consolidation and infrastructure hardening, culminating in the migration of Eliza Cloud to `eliza.app`. The team successfully stabilized the agent framework by optimizing cold-start latencies, implementing robust SSRF media guards, and promoting "phone-first" personal agent features to production. These efforts, supported by significant CI/CD workflow refinements and the integration of the `smthrs` visual workflow studio, have significantly improved the reliability and scalability of the ElizaOS ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Key Developments

### Cloud Infrastructure & Consolidation
- **Platform Unification:** The team completed the canonical consolidation of Eliza Cloud into `eliza.app`, streamlining login and account management ([#18996](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18996)).
- **Performance & Scaling:** Agent cold-start latency was significantly reduced via server-side prewarming for shared-runtime turn caches ([#18762](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18762)). Node capacity management was improved by sizing based on hardware specifications rather than global variables ([#18639](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18639)).
- **Voice & Media:** Realtime voice features, including barge-in support and dynamic greetings, were promoted to production ([#19655](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/19655)). Media reliability was bolstered by implementing retry logic for cold-cache warming ([#18323](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18323)).

### Agent Orchestration & Workflow
- **Visual Workflow Studio:** The native `smthrs` studio was integrated, providing a visual interface for workflow editing ([#19000](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/19000)). The runtime was further upgraded to `smthrs` 0.34.0, utilizing React Flow for a more compact dependency stack ([#19420](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/19420)).
- **Core Logic:** Planner reliability was enhanced by enforcing turn-scope declarations ([#18247](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18247)) and preventing internal guidance text from leaking into chat outputs ([#18315](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18315)).

### Security & Hygiene
- **SSRF Protection:** To prevent security vulnerabilities, image and audio fetches were routed through shared SSRF media guards across multiple plugins, including `plugin-openai` and `plugin-google-genai` ([#18716](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18716)).
- **Dependency Patching:** A critical security advisory in `pdfjs-dist` was addressed by pinning to version 6.2.108 ([#18873](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/18873)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage

### Closed Issues
The project saw a high volume of resolutions regarding CI/CD stability and cloud provisioning. Key fixes included:
- **CI/CD:** Retirement of orphaned workflows and resolution of CI gate timeouts ([#16272](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/16272), [#17121](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17121)).
- **Cloud Stability:** Resolution of provisioning failures, identity-handoff issues, and agent trajectory accounting errors ([#17154](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17154), [#17532](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17532)).

### New & Active Issues
- **Cloud Warm Pool ([#16961](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/16961)):** Ongoing audit of image-drift and daemon-owned rollout phases remains a priority, with a focus on digest-aware rollout status.
- **Auth Latency ([#18049](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/18049)):** Investigation continues into multi-second provider lookup spikes, which remain a concern for onboarding bootstrap performance.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
The week was characterized by high-velocity maintenance and infrastructure hardening. The volume of PRs—particularly in the "Core & Plugins" and "Cloud & Auth" categories—indicates a highly active contributor base focused on stabilizing the framework for production use. The frequent promotion of `develop` to `main` throughout the week demonstrates a disciplined approach to maintaining production stability while rapidly iterating on new features like the `smthrs` studio and voice integration.
