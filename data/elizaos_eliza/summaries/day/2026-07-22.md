# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Jul 22, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on July 22, 2026, focused on stabilizing the `develop` branch by resolving critical CI regressions, synchronizing toolchain versions, and hardening cloud/voice infrastructure.
