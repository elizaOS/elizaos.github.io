# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Aug 1, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
August 1, 2026, was a productive day for ElizaOS, marked by the successful launch of the `eliza.army` contribution skill and leaderboard. Development focused on stabilizing cloud infrastructure, improving core provider reliability, and refining CI/CD pipelines to ensure robust cross-platform performance.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

**Contribution & Ecosystem Launch**
* Launched the `eliza.army` contribution skill and leaderboard, enabling community members to track and earn for open-source contributions ([#17424](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17424)).

**Cloud & Agent Runtime Stability**
* Implemented safety alignment for cloud snapshot retain budgets ([#17180](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17180)).

**CI/CD & Infrastructure Improvements**
* Optimized Windows CI by using runtime-native contract entrypoints ([#17526](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17526)).
