# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Sep 2, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on Sep 2, 2026, focused heavily on stabilizing the cloud infrastructure, refining billing and subscription policies, and optimizing inference admission. Major achievements include the implementation of a robust subscription funding taxonomy, significant latency reductions in inference admission, and the successful promotion of certified develop builds to production.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

**Cloud Billing & Subscription Lifecycle**
*   Implemented a new subscription funding policy, defining a taxonomy for allowance-eligible versus cash-only usage ([#30165](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30165)).
*   Enabled reservation of subscription allowances before utilizing purchased credits, ensuring efficient resource management ([#30166](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30166)).
*   Persisted subscription lifecycle aggregates and allowance authority with immutable revision history ([#30154](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30154)).
*   Added reporting for uncollected subscription overages to improve billing transparency ([#30167](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30167)).

**Inference Admission & Proxy Optimization**
*   Reduced inference admission latency by removing redundant pre-auth rate limiter calls and optimizing the authoritative per-organization gate ([#30351](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30351)).
*   Fused generative credential and balance admission to resolve caller identity once across proxy engines ([#30265](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30265)).
*   Guarded paid proxy routes with combined admission to ensure secure and efficient access ([#30264](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30264)).

**Infrastructure Stability & CI/CD**
*   Isolated Dedicated startup from Shared runtime to prevent provisioning conflicts ([#29973](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29973)).
*   Restored and repaired develop integration contracts and workflow coverage across multiple platforms ([#30363](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30363), [#30342](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30342), [#30330](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30330)).
*   Promoted certified develop build 769d433 to production following successful A/B placement certification ([#30366](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30366), [#30107](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30107)).
