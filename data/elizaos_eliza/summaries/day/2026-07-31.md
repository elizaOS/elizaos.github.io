# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Jul 31, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on July 31, 2026, focused on stabilizing the framework's cloud infrastructure, improving agent coordination, and refining the UI/UX. Major efforts included resolving critical production deletion failures, enhancing CI reliability, and porting the eliza-app frontend to the monorepo homepage.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### Cloud Infrastructure & Reliability
*   **Deletion & Recovery:** Addressed critical production failures in agent deletion by removing redundant read-modify-write operations on timestamps ([#17249](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17249)). Improved job recovery by ensuring dependent rows settle when jobs fail ([#17442](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17442)) and preventing stranded warm-pool rows from being counted as ready capacity ([#17286](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17286)).
*   **Orphan Management:** Fixed a bug where the orphan reaper incorrectly deleted live warm-claimed containers by keying them on physical names ([#17256](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17256)).

### Agent Coordination & Core Logic
*   **Discord Coordination:** Implemented P4 group-room coordination to manage speaker leases and prevent multiple agents from responding to the same message ([#17213](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17213)).
*   **Provenance & Safety:** Introduced a canonical provenance envelope with trusted scope stamping ([#17304](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17304)) and enforced single-voice delivery contracts across the action surface to prevent duplicate responses ([#17478](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17478)).

### UI/UX & Homepage
*   **Homepage Port:** Successfully ported the standalone `eliza-app` frontend into the monorepo Vite homepage, deploying it to Cloudflare ([#17376](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17376)).
*   **Visual Enhancements:** Added native chat glass effects for iOS ([#17048](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17048)) and updated fresh installs to use the "Ember Night" wallpaper by default ([#17454](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/17454)).

## ACTIVE ISSUES

*   **[#13562](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/13562) (Flawless testing loop):** The nightly "Views Soak" continues to fail, indicating potential render-storms or memory growth in the `ViewRouter`. Recent comments highlight that these failures are being tracked to ensure the testing matrix eventually reaches a "flawless" state.
*   **[#17136](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17136) (Nightly Live Scenarios):** After 19 consecutive nights of failures, recent changes have successfully split the lane into parallel shards. Discussions confirm that the janitor exemption and raised ceilings are working, though the `lifeops-connectors` shard remains a performance bottleneck that may require further sub-splitting.
*   **[#17249](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17249) (Agent Deletion Failures):** Production audits confirmed that agent deletion was failing due to type mismatches in the deletion retry path. While the fix is merged to main, active discussions are monitoring the drain of the remaining stuck agents in production.
*   **[#17421](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/issues/17421) (Runner Infrastructure):** Extensive discussion regarding I/O starvation and port collisions on self-hosted runners. Maintainers are actively identifying and draining "sick" hosts (e.g., `runner-12`, `prod-robot-2`) while implementing per-runner home directories to prevent race conditions.
