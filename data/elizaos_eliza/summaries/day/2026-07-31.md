# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Jul 31, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on July 31, 2026, focused on stabilizing the AI agent framework through critical infrastructure hardening, CI/CD reliability, and UI/UX refinements. Key achievements include the implementation of native iOS glass effects, Discord group-room coordination, and significant cloud provisioning fixes. The team also addressed persistent CI failures and improved the reliability of the eliza.app deployment pipeline.
