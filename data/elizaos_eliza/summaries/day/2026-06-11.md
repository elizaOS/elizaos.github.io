# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Jun 11, 2026)

Development on June 11, 2026, focused on stabilizing the agent orchestrator, enhancing cloud infrastructure, and expanding the plugin ecosystem. *   Enabled autonomous sub-agent dispatching by wiring the `USE_SKILL` broker, allowing agents to drive monetized-app loops ([#8275](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/8275)).
