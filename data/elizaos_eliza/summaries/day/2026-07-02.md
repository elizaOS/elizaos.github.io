# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Jul 2, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on July 2, 2026, focused on hardening the ElizaOS framework through extensive security, financial integrity, and UI/UX refinements. Key achievements include implementing vendor-neutral model gateways, finalizing the Gemma-4 benchmark cutover, and resolving critical money-path bugs in cloud billing and credit reconciliation. The team also completed a major UI interaction epic and restored significant test coverage lost in previous rebase operations.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

**Cloud Financial Integrity & Security**
*   Implemented vendor-neutral model-gateway configurations to protect raw provider credentials ([#11660](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11660), [#11651](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11651)).
*   Resolved critical money-path issues, including idempotent credit reconciliation, fixing double-refund bugs in app-chat streams, and gating provisioning on credit availability ([#11606](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11606), [#11686](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11686), [#11679](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11679)).
*   Hardened security by encrypting sandbox environment secrets at rest and implementing SSRF fetch-guard pinning ([#11402](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11402), [#11178](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11178)).

**Orchestrator & Agent Intelligence**
*   Advanced the orchestrator with model-backed sub-agent interruption decisions and improved task-store concurrency ([#11210](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11210), [#11197](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11197)).
*   Completed the Gemma-4-31b benchmark cutover and integrated Android OCR via ML Kit Text Recognition v2 ([#11196](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11196), [#11111](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11111)).
*   Refined coding-backend routing and interactive PTY vendor CLI support ([#11582](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11582), [#11623](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11623)).

**UI/UX & Platform Polish**
*   Completed a major UI interaction epic, including a simplified launcher, programmable GLSL shader backgrounds, and improved touch-gesture handling ([#11174](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11174), [#11083](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11083), [#11334](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11334)).
*   Enhanced the `eliza-code` TUI with markdown rendering, clipboard support, and active model status indicators ([#11591](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11591), [#11479](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11479), [#11476](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/11476)).
