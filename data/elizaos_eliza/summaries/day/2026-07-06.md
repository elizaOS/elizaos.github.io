# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Jul 6, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on July 6, 2026, focused on stabilizing the ElizaOS framework for demo readiness. Key achievements include a major UI/UX overhaul for chat and notification surfaces, the implementation of a robust evidence-certification protocol, and critical fixes for cloud provisioning and mobile app stability.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

### UI/UX Refinement & Chat Chrome
*   **Chat & Notification Overhaul:** Implemented top-glass notification banners, inline home widgets, and a redesigned notification shade. Chat now features a "humanness" voice gate for outbound messages and improved reply-to-message context. ([#15153](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15153), [#15114](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15114), [#15080](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15080), [#14923](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/14923), [#15050](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15050))
*   **Onboarding & Launcher:** Streamlined the first-run experience with silent cloud entry for returning users and a new curated wallpaper gallery. ([#15149](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15149), [#15042](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15042))

### Evidence Certification & Observability
*   **Certification Protocol:** Finalized the one-command certification orchestrator, enabling Ed25519-signed verdict rollups and offline verification for evidence bundles. ([#15004](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15004), [#14610](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/14610))
*   **GPU Vision & Analysis:** Deployed a filesystem-based GPU vision job queue and a tiered heuristic analyzer registry for automated evidence processing. ([#15006](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15006), [#14611](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/14611))

### LifeOps & Personal Assistant
*   **Commitment & Proactivity:** Introduced a durable commitment ledger primitive and model-decided proactivity gates to manage owner-facing interactions. ([#15086](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15086), [#14931](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/14931))
*   **Corpus Tooling:** Added private workspace tooling (`@elizaos/corpus-tools`) for deterministic PII mining and scrubbing to ensure privacy in agent training. ([#15058](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15058), [#15074](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15074))

### Cloud & Infrastructure Stability
*   **Provisioning Fixes:** Resolved critical launch-blockers in the provisioning worker, including SIGTERM handling and syntax errors in pre-pull commands. ([#15158](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15158), [#15123](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/15123))
