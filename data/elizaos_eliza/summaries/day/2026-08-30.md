# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Aug 30, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on August 30, 2026, focused heavily on hardening the ElizaOS cloud infrastructure. Key achievements include the implementation of a durable backup admission authority, improved billing and account deletion consistency, and significant stabilization of CI/CD workflows and latency certification processes.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

**Cloud Infrastructure & Backup Systems**
*   Established a durable foundation for agent backup admission, including schema migrations and GC authority ([#29662](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29662)).
*   Implemented repository-level enrollment for backup work by durable shard ([#29694](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29694)).
*   Added bounded claimant/recovery authority to manage backup work without shard starvation ([#29781](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29781)).

**Billing & Account Management Hardening**
*   Ensured billing cancellation receipts are preserved across account deletion events ([#29651](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29651)).
*   Enforced unified account-standing decisions across inference and paid routes to ensure fail-closed security ([#29984](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29984), [#29977](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29977)).

**CI/CD, Testing, and Workflow Stability**
*   Restored deterministic contracts for HMR, runtime, and visual/OCR tests ([#30071](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30071)).
*   Implemented staging latency certification workflows and improved staging selection re-review operators ([#30044](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30044), [#30048](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30048)).
*   Refined staging selection bootstrap blockers and neutral reporting ([#30100](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30100), [#30108](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30108)).
