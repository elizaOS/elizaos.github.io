# elizaos/eliza Daily Update (Sep 1, 2026)

## OVERVIEW 
Development on September 1, 2026, focused heavily on the "Sandboxes V2" epic, with significant advancements in cloud infrastructure, backup-restore reliability, and inference admission security. The team also delivered critical UI/UX refinements for the calendar plugin and resolved several authentication and CI/CD pipeline issues to ensure system stability across Web2 and Web3 environments.

## KEY TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENTS

**Cloud Infrastructure & Sandbox V2 Enhancements**
*   Implemented atomic backup-admission reservations and durable restore streaming with cryptographic boundaries ([#29782](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29782), [#29950](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29950), [#29949](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29949)).
*   Added quarantined Docker restore capabilities and composed exact restore/cleanup workflows ([#29928](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29928), [#29929](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29929)).
*   Hardened secret transport for restores and reconciled capacity management for replacement attempts ([#29925](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29925), [#29927](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29927)).

**Authentication, Security & Inference Admission**
*   Optimized inference admission by fusing revocation checks with lease management and awaiting authoritative pricing on cache misses ([#30096](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30096), [#30105](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30105)).
*   Improved auth session persistence and gated paid routes by standing to ensure secure, authenticated access ([#29600](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29600), [#30267](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30267)).
*   Enforced strict identity verification for X (Twitter) and Telegram integrations ([#29595](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29595), [#29767](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/29767)).

**UI/UX & Plugin Refinements**
*   Resolved responsive layout regressions in the calendar plugin, ensuring the event editor and month grid remain within viewport boundaries ([#30251](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30251), [#30232](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30232), [#30234](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30234)).
*   Improved iMessage transport reporting and canonical date handling for all-day events ([#30248](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30248), [#30240](https://github.com/elizaos/eliza/pull/30240)).
